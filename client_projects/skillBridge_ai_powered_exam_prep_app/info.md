SkillBridge – AI-Powered Exam Prep App
My role. AI Software Engineer

Project description.

We built SkillBridge, an AI-powered exam prep platform for high school students across Africa. The app features curriculum-aligned notes, personalized mock exams, an AI tutor chatbot, and gamified quizzes that adapt to each student's learning pace. Launched on both Android and iOS, it achieved 1,000+ downloads within its first release cycle and expanded its reach to students across 20+ African countries making quality education accessible continent-wide.
